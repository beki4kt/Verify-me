CHEKMI Support

Help with your workspace.

Signed-in users can open and track a private support case from Help & privacy in the app.

Email support

Email support@chekmi.app. Include the workspace code, your role, and a concise description. Never send passwords, one-time codes, API keys, or full receipt images by email.

Payment issue

Include the payment provider, transaction reference, approximate time, and the status shown in CHEKMI. Contact the payment provider directly for settlement or account disputes.

Privacy and deletion

Email privacy@chekmi.app for privacy requests, or follow the account deletion guide.
