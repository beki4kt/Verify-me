Account control

Delete your CHEKMI account.

You can permanently delete a staff account from the app or request help without reinstalling the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to your organization workspace.
  2. Open Help & privacy.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Enter a reason, confirm the warning, and choose Request account deletion.

The staff profile is anonymized immediately, credentials are destroyed, active sessions are revoked, and the user is signed out.

Request deletion outside the app

Email privacy@chekmi.app with the subject “CHEKMI account deletion request.” Include your workspace code and work phone number so we can verify the request privately. Do not send a password. We will confirm identity before processing.

What is deleted

The staff name, work phone number, password credential, and live sessions are removed or irreversibly replaced. Support content that is no longer required is deleted under the applicable retention schedule.

What may be retained

Payment and financial audit rows may retain an anonymous staff reference for up to seven years where required for accounting, disputes, security, fraud prevention, or law. The retained reference cannot be used to sign in and no longer contains the deleted user’s name or phone number.

Business workspace closure

A business administrator can choose Request business closure on the same screen. CHEKMI reviews business closure requests against contract and financial retention duties before deleting eligible workspace data.
