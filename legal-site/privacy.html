Privacy notice

This notice explains how CHEKMI processes information for restaurant workspaces, staff accounts, payment verification, and support.

Information we process

We process business details and configuration; staff name, work phone number, role, and account credentials; payment provider, transaction reference, expected and verified amounts, timestamps, and receipt evidence; device and security events; and information submitted to support.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate staff, isolate business workspaces, verify payment references, prevent duplicate or unauthorized transactions, provide operational records, respond to support, secure the service, and meet legal obligations.

Legal basis and business instructions

CHEKMI processes staff and transaction information to provide the contracted business service, protect the service and its users, meet legal duties, and act on the instructions of the business that provides the workspace. Businesses are responsible for telling staff and customers how their information is used.

Service providers and transfers

We use infrastructure, database, payment verification, and support providers to operate CHEKMI. They receive only the information needed for their service. We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed outside Ethiopia where a provider operates, subject to contractual and technical safeguards.

Retention

Receipt evidence is normally retained for 365 days. Transaction and financial audit records may be retained for up to seven years when required for accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, or law. Support and security records are retained only as long as needed for their purpose. An individual staff account deletion immediately removes the staff name, phone number, credential, and active sessions; financial rows retain an anonymous staff reference.

Security

CHEKMI uses encrypted transport, tenant-scoped access, hashed credentials, short-lived sessions, restricted service credentials, audit logs, and recovery procedures. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access or correct personal information through your business administrator or CHEKMI Support. You can delete your staff account inside the app under Help & privacy → Account. A business administrator can also request workspace closure. See the deletion guide.

Children

CHEKMI is a workplace service and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

We will update the effective date when this notice changes. Contact privacy@chekmi.app for privacy requests or CHEKMI Support for product help.
